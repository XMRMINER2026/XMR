Understanding XMR

Discover the world of Monero

Learn what makes Monero different and how it approaches privacy in digital payments.

A cryptocurrency built around financial privacy

Monero (XMR) is an open-source, decentralized cryptocurrency launched in 2014. Its network is maintained by participants around the world, rather than controlled by a single company.

Unlike transparent blockchains where transaction details can often be publicly traced, Monero uses privacy technologies to conceal the sender, recipient, and amount of a transaction by default.

Monero is designed to make individual transactions difficult to link together, helping users maintain greater confidentiality when using digital money.

What does XMR mean?

XMR is the currency ticker for Monero. It is the unit used to represent value transferred across the Monero network. Like other cryptocurrencies, its market price can fluctuate.


Is Monero anonymous?

Monero provides strong on-chain privacy protections, but no technology guarantees complete anonymity in every situation. Device security, wallet practices, and how you access the internet still matter.
